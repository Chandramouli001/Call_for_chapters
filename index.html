About the Book

TinyML-Enabled IoT Systems: Recent Advances is a peer-reviewed edited volume published by Bentham Science Publishers and indexed in Scopus. It targets researchers, engineers, and practitioners working at the intersection of machine learning and constrained embedded systems.

The book solicits original, unpublished chapters spanning foundational theory, system architectures, algorithmic innovation, deployment pipelines, and real-world application domains — from industrial IoT and precision agriculture to smart healthcare and security-critical embedded AI.

There are no publication charges. Chapters are subject to rigorous peer review, and the corresponding author must hold an H-Index greater than 10. Submitted manuscripts must maintain plagiarism below 10% and AI-similarity at 0%.
