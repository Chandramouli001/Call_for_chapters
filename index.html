About the Book

Advancing Edge Intelligence

A curated collection of research and real-world applications at the intersection of machine learning and IoT.

Tiny Machine Learning (TinyML) enables intelligent ML models to run directly on ultra-low-power microcontrollers and edge IoT devices — bringing AI closer to the physical world without cloud dependency.

This edited book presents cutting-edge contributions across healthcare, smart agriculture, wearable devices, industrial IoT, and beyond — bringing together researchers and practitioners from around the world.

Aim & Scope

Why This Book Matters

This volume provides a platform for researchers, academicians, and industry professionals to share innovative architectures, deployment strategies, and real-world implementations of TinyML-enabled IoT systems. With a focus on resource-constrained embedded devices, the book bridges the gap between machine learning theory and practical edge deployment — covering everything from model compression to secure inference at the network edge.

Guidelines

Submission Guidelines

Everything you need to know before submitting your chapter.

Author Guidelines

  • Chapter must be original and unpublished work
  • Similarity index must be below 15%
  • AI-generated content must be 0%
  • Minimum chapter length: more than 10 pages
  • Maximum number of authors: 6
  • Figures and tables must be clear and high resolution
  • Submit in Microsoft Word (.doc / .docx)

Submission Procedure

  • Download chapter template from this website
  • Submit abstract (max 250 words) via email or Google Form
  • Receive abstract acceptance notification
  • Submit full chapter after acceptance
  • Chapter undergoes editorial review process
  • Submit camera-ready chapter with plagiarism and AI reports

Author Benefits

All chapters undergo peer review. The book will have an ISBN and each chapter will receive a DOI through Zenodo, indexed in OpenAIRE. Distributed globally through major platforms with one complimentary hard copy provided to authors.
