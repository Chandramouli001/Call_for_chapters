About

About the book

This peer-reviewed edited volume targets researchers, engineers, and practitioners working at the intersection of machine learning and constrained embedded systems.

We're looking for original, unpublished chapters spanning theory, system architecture, algorithms, deployment, and real-world applications — from industrial IoT and precision agriculture to smart healthcare and security-critical embedded AI.
